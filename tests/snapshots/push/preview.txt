---- Before pushing, consider ----
- Changing the VTP domain resets the revision number; in client/server mode the switch stops syncing with the previous domain.
- SNMP: the communities and trap destinations not listed here are deleted. Monitoring that uses another community will stop receiving data.
- NTP: the servers not listed here are deleted.
- Syslog: the servers not listed here are deleted.
- VTY SSH only: telnet is disabled. Switches without RSA keys or without "ip ssh" would lose remote access (the SSH sweep already confirms SSH works on the ones it connects to).
- access-class MANAGEMENT on the VTYs: if the ACL does not permit the IP of your computer, your VPN or the backup server, remote access is lost (the current session continues). Check the ACL first.
- No HTTP/HTTPS server: on the 9200 the WebUI is turned off.
- Changing the spanning-tree mode to rapid-pvst restarts STP on all VLANs: 1 to 50 second outages on each switch. Do it in a maintenance window and starting with the root.
- The user password ******** enable is changed on all switches. Record it in the password ******** before saving.
- The additional commands touch remote access (vty, AAA, ACL, SVI, SSH or users): review them twice, a mistake can lock you out of the switches.
